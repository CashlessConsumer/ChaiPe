📝 Demo Article

Welcome to the ChaiPe interactive playground! This is a sample article to demonstrate behavioral nudging in action. Scroll down to see how ChaiPe tracks your engagement and shows the tip modal at the right moment.

ChaiPe uses smart heuristics to determine when to show the payment prompt. It considers:

  • Scroll Depth: How far you've read through the content
  • Time on Page: How long you've been engaged with the article
  • User Interactions: Clicks, selections, and other engagement signals
  • Return Visitor: Whether you've visited before (localStorage)

The key insight is that people are most likely to tip after they've received value. Showing a payment popup immediately when someone lands on your page is intrusive and counterproductive. But showing it after they've read 70% of your article or spent 2 minutes reading? That's when conversion rates are highest.

Try it out: Configure the settings on the left, then either scroll down to trigger the automatic nudge, or click "Show Modal" to see it immediately. The console in the bottom-left corner shows real-time events and behavioral tracking.
